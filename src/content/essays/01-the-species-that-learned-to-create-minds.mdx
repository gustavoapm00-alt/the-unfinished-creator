For millennia, humans imagined making beings in their own image. Today, machine learning systems force us to ask which parts of intelligence can be engineered—and which parts we do not yet understand.

This essay will distinguish technical capability from subjective consciousness, examine historical creation narratives without treating them as predictions, and ask whether wisdom can keep pace with invention.

## Research agenda

1. Define intelligence, consciousness, agency, and wisdom without conflating them.
2. Establish what current AI systems can reliably do, with primary technical evidence.
3. Examine ancient creation narratives in their historical contexts.
4. Explain the ethical significance of creating systems whose behavior we cannot fully predict.

*This is a research outline, not a completed essay.*
